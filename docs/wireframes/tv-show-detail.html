Breaking Bad

9.5
2008-2013 5 Seasons TV-MA
Crime Drama Thriller
Watch Now

Synopsis

A high school chemistry teacher diagnosed with inoperable lung cancer turns to manufacturing and selling methamphetamine in order to secure his family's future. As he descends deeper into the criminal underworld, his transformation from mild-mannered educator to ruthless drug kingpin challenges everything he believes about himself.

Created by Vince Gilligan, Breaking Bad is widely regarded as one of the greatest television series of all time, praised for its acting, writing, directing, and character development.
